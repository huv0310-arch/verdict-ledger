AI 干活要有账，AI 改自己也要有账。

## English version (GitHub front page, 144 words)

**Verdict Ledger** — the ledgers behind AI judgment and self-improvement.

The EU AI Act entered its enforcement phase on 2026-08-02, and the draft fining-procedure rules close for public comment on 11-09 — auditable AI judgment is timely now. Hash-chain audits from others only prove the log was never altered; we go one step further: correct verdicts are provable, wrong verdicts can be appealed.
Two pieces ship together: V2 ("Plexus") builds a court for every judgment AI makes; V3 (Contra) an inspection room for AI rewriting itself — pure stdlib, no dependencies.
V2 records verdicts append-only; appeals are stamped, history never deleted. V3 (Contra) logs each self-modification: what, why, how it went.
Install in three lines — see README.
Honest limits: fail-open when it breaks; the hash chain stops single-sided tampering, not collusion.
Doing AI's work needs a ledger; AI changing itself needs one too.
